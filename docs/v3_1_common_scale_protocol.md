# v3.1 common-scale availability amendment

Registered before new system photometry, predictions or residuals. Targeted
availability checks: NGC1261, NGC1851 and NGC7492, spanning approximately12–24kpc.
The historical giant and K-giant inventories contain their published membership
counts but zero matched MWS spectra/qualified giants. Inventory is not a previous
photdis distance test. Audit all actual historical input identities/footprints
again; any member exposure quarantines the whole system. No final role yet.

Require published Vasiliev/Baumgardt member probability>.9, exact positive G3
identity/primary native DR11 owner, TYPE=PSF and the unchanged photometric/EBV/
latitude cuts. Missing/nonPSF and all failed photometric entries remain counted.
Do not relax EBV<.03 to admit a particular cluster. Preserve membership selection
dependence on Gaia astrometry and distinguish it from an independent scale.

For availability and eventual pre-frozen scale tests, prefer the HST kinematic
columns of BV21 Table2 for NGC1261 and NGC1851, rather than their combined values:
16.775±.872kpc and11.440±.320kpc. These uncertainties already include the paper's
additional1.7%term; do not add it again. Model shared distance uncertainty once
per cluster. Treat a common±3%method-scale sensitivity across the HST systems
separately; it is a robustness scenario, not a measured zero-point correction.
The method fits HST proper-motion and line-of-sight dispersions with dynamical
models, retaining anisotropy/mass-function and velocity-source dependencies.
NGC7492 has only a literature-mixture distance in this table; its24.434kpc
value with−.567/+.581kpc errors is availability metadata, not certified
independent absolute truth. Component literature ancestry must be checked before
any stronger use. [BV21, sections2.3/4.1 and Table2](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.09526v2)

No prospective correction may train on these objects. Freeze actual PSF IDs,
reference definitions and correlated-error treatment after qualification and
before fitting a correction; open final residuals only after candidate freeze.
Empty admitted systems remain explicit failures of evidence availability.
